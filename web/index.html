Resolution mode

Syntax and scope

Enter the left-hand side of f = 0 in the chosen variables with rational coefficients, as in (1/2)*x^3 + y^2. The germ is taken at the origin.

Embedded resolution keeps the exceptional divisor in the ambient space. Intrinsic resolution records the exceptional locus on the smooth model of the hypersurface. Components are taken over the algebraic closure of ℚ.

If the quick calculation is inconclusive, an exhaustive search continues in the background. Select Exhaustive search to start one directly. You can cancel it while it runs.
