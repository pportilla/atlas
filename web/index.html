Syntax and scope

Write the left-hand side of f = 0 with rational coefficients, using * for products, ^ for powers and parentheses, as in (1/2)*x^3 + y^2. The germ is taken at the origin.

Embedded resolves the hypersurface inside the ambient space; the dual complex is that of the ambient exceptional divisor. Intrinsic resolves the hypersurface itself; the dual complex is that of the exceptional locus on the resolved hypersurface. Components are taken over the algebraic closure of ℚ.

The certificate is local at the origin and the resolution is not assumed to be minimal. If the quick resolver is inconclusive, an exhaustive search continues in the background until it finishes or is cancelled.
