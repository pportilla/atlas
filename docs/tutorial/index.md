# Atlas tutorial

This tutorial is the best way to become familiar with Atlas. You can work through it at the prompt in a few hours. Each chapter poses mathematical questions and answers them with short calculations. Read the chapters in order the first time. Every section declares the coordinates it needs, so you can later return to a single section in a fresh session.

You need a working copy of Atlas. [Installation guide](../installation.md)

1. [The prompt and scripts](prompt-and-scripts.md). Enter a calculation, save it as a script and ask for help on a saved value.
2. [Rings, coordinates and maps](rings.md). Declare polynomial and quotient rings, name and select rings, and move polynomials between rings.
3. [Polynomials and ideals](polynomials-and-ideals.md). Factor, divide and find common factors of polynomials, then reduce modulo an ideal, eliminate, intersect, saturate, decompose and raise ideals to powers. Exact linear and modular arithmetic close the chapter.
4. [Quotient rings, localization and normalization](quotient-rings.md). Compute with residue classes, localize at a prime, normalize the cusp and read a graded Hilbert series.
5. [Local algebras and map germs](local-algebra.md). Count lengths at a point, compute Milnor and Tjurina numbers and the codimension of a map germ, and reduce at a rational point.
6. [Geometry at a rational point](local-geometry.md). Compare local dimension and smoothness, tangent spaces, tangent cones and the Hilbert–Samuel function.
7. [Resolving a plane cusp](resolving-a-cusp.md). Follow the branch of the cusp through three blowups, read the multiplicity of a divisor in a chart, and compute the topological and monodromy zeta functions of the cusp.

After the tutorial, the [thematic tutorials](../thematic/index.md) treat zeta functions, the monodromy conjecture and resolution records in depth. [Constructions](../constructions.md) answer short “How do I…?” questions, and the [reference manual](../reference/index.md) documents every function.
