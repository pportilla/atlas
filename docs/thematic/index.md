# Thematic tutorials

Each of these tutorials treats one subject in depth. They assume the [tutorial](../tutorial/index.md), in particular its chapter on [resolving a plane cusp](../tutorial/resolving-a-cusp.md). Every section starts from an empty session, so you can read them in any order.

## Resolution of singularities

- [Zeta functions and the monodromy conjecture](zeta-functions.md). Compute the local topological and monodromy zeta functions of a germ, see where each term comes from, and check that every pole gives a monodromy eigenvalue at or near the origin.
- [Resolution records](resolution-records.md). Save a resolution from the shell and compute from it later, compare embedded and intrinsic resolutions, read charts and dual complexes, and use the Resolution Zoo catalogue.
- [How Atlas resolves a hypersurface germ](resolution-pipeline.md). How Atlas chooses a modification, constructs and checks its charts, and what a saved record certifies.
- [Embedded resolution by weighted blowups](weighted-resolution.md). Resolve $x^2+y^3+z^5$ with a single weighted blowup and read its quotient charts.
- [Resolving surfaces by Jung's method](jung-method.md). Resolve a surface singularity through the branch curve of a generic projection, and read the curves of its minimal good resolution.
