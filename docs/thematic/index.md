# Thematic tutorials

Each of these tutorials treats one subject in depth. They assume the [tutorial](../tutorial/index.md), in particular its chapter on [resolving a plane cusp](../tutorial/resolving-a-cusp.md).

## Resolution of singularities

- [Reading a resolution record](resolution-records.md). Save and replay a resolution, choose between embedded and intrinsic modes, and compute the local monodromy and topological zeta functions of the cusp.
- [How Atlas resolves a hypersurface germ](resolution-pipeline.md). How Atlas chooses a modification, constructs and checks its charts, and what a saved record certifies.
- [Embedded resolution by weighted blowups](weighted-resolution.md). Resolve $x^2+y^3+z^5$ with a single weighted blowup and read its quotient charts.
